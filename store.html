How It Works

From idea to jersey.

  1. 01

    Tell Us What You Need

    Message us on WhatsApp or use the contact form with your team name, colours, squad size and any logos you want included.

  2. 02

    Design Proof

    We put together a design mockup for your review before anything goes into production.

  3. 03

    Sizing & Quote

    Once the design is approved, confirm sizes per player and we'll give you a final price for the full order.

  4. 04

    Production

    Your jerseys are printed and finished at our studio using durable, wash-resistant materials.

  5. 05

    Collection Or Delivery

    Collect from Kampala, Koch Goma or our Gulu Main Market studio, or arrange delivery for teams outside the region.
