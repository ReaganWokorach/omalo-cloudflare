By Sector

What each sector typically comes to us for.

Corporate Sector: logos, brand identity, business stationery, office branding and marketing materials.

Government Sector: institutional signage, public information materials, banners and official documentation printing.

Education Sector: prospectuses, certificates, ID cards, banners and campus signage.

Non Governmental Sector: campaign materials, reports, banners, IEC materials and event branding.

Religious Sector: programme booklets, banners, branded apparel and event materials.

Events Sector: backdrops, stage branding, invitations and delegate materials.

Individuals: personal cards, invitations, portraits, small prints and personal branding.

Where We Work

Based in Kampala and Gulu City. Working across Uganda and beyond.

Omalo Graphics Centre Ltd has locations in Kampala and Koch Goma, Gulu City, and serves clients across the country from those bases. Government offices, NGOs, schools, churches and businesses bring their design, branding and print work to our studios.

We also work with clients outside the region, who send artwork and instructions remotely and receive finished work through an agreed arrangement. Distance does not change the standard we hold ourselves to.
